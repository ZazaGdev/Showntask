# Summarise a Showntask recording

Recording: {{title}} (id `{{id}}`, {{length}}).

Read the timed transcript with `get_recording` (or `get_transcript` in spans when it is long).
Look at a frame with `get_frame` only when the talk depends on what is on screen.

Write:

1. A summary of 3-8 sentences: what the recording is about and what happened.
2. **Decisions**: each decision taken, with its time (mm:ss).
3. **Open questions**: what was left unanswered, with its time.

Use only what was said and shown. Never use the em dash or the en dash.
